Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 25, 2026

Key Summary: TokGallery operates completely on your device. We do not collect, upload, transmit, track, or sell your photos, videos, audio files, or personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to TokGallery ("we", "our", or "the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how TokGallery handles permissions and device storage when you use the app.

2. Information We Access & How It Is Used

TokGallery is an offline-first, vertical video and media viewer for content already stored on your device. To provide its core features, the app requests the following Android permissions:

  • Read Media Videos / Photos (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used solely to discover and play your local videos and display photos inside the immersive feed and grid gallery.
  • Read Media Audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Used if you choose to select and overlay custom background music from your device's audio library.

All media indexing, thumbnail generation, video decoding, and audio playback take place 100% locally on your device. None of your media files are ever uploaded to any external server or cloud service.

3. Local Storage & User Preferences

To improve your playback experience, TokGallery stores lightweight app preferences locally on your device (using Android SharedPreferences), including:

  • Your last played video position (so you can resume where you left off).
  • Local like and bookmark markers.
  • Audio volume, playback speeds, and soundtrack filter choices.

This data remains stored locally on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app storage.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

TokGallery does not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or user behavior analytics. We do not sell, rent, or monetize any user data.

5. Children’s Privacy

TokGallery does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, our app is safe for general audiences.

6. Security

Because TokGallery processes all data locally without transmitting information over the internet, your personal media remains under your direct physical and operating system security controls.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
